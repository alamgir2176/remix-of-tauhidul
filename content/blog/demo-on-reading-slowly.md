This is **demo text** written only to show how a published article looks. Replace it with your own writing. Philosophy taught me to pause before deciding what something means, and that habit changes how a page is read.

## Why slow reading?

Reading slowly is not about speed. It is about *attention*. A few things that help:

- Read the sentence twice before moving on.
- Mark the words you do not fully understand.
- Write one question in the margin of every page.

> A good question is a door, not a wall.

### A small routine

1. Choose one short text.
2. Read it aloud once.
3. Write three sentences in your own words.
